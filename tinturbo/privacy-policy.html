Tinturbo privacy policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Tinturbo is a game by Fulico Games. This page explains which data is used, by whom and why.

In short

  • You sign in with a Google account. We keep your progress on a server so it is not lost.
  • Your in-game name, Fred, level, frame, score and challenge rating appear in the ranking and in challenges to other players, who can also open your public profile.
  • Detailed game data is only sent if you accept, and you can stop at any time.
  • You can delete your account inside the game.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads, which collect some device data.
  • Ads only appear when you choose to watch them.

Account and progress

When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google an identifier and your public email, name and photo. We only use them to recognize your account; none of them appear in the game. We also keep:

  • the name you choose in the game (unique among players);
  • progress: level, golden paws, purchases, achievements, skills, statistics, rating and challenges;
  • your games, drop by drop, with the game version used, to check the score and prevent cheating.

The ranking shows other players your in-game name, Fred, level, frame and score. Without a chosen name, you do not appear.

If you choose a name, other players with an account can see your public profile from the ranking: Fred, name, level, rating, game statistics, Shenanigans and achievements. Your paw balance, energy, purchases and usage data never appear.

You can add friends by name; whoever receives the request sees your public profile. Your friend list and blocks are not shown to other players.

Challenges and prizes. When you post a challenge or challenge someone, other players see your name, Fred, rating, the score to beat, the Shenanigans used in the game and the stake in paws. At the end, those who took part see the result. Opponent matching uses your rating and whether you played in the last few days. To keep challenges fair, we keep for 7 days the duels you declined or let expire. They can suspend your challenges for 24 hours and are not shown to anyone. In the previous ranking result, the paws won by each position are shown.

Detailed game data (optional)

If you accept, we also receive: touches (including outside the buttons) and timings of each game, whether it started offline and whether its delivery to the server worked, pauses, screens and buttons used, purchases and achievements, and the model, system, screen, language and volume settings of your device. It is only used to balance and improve the game. It is linked to your account, is not sold or used for ads, and is kept for up to 12 months. The colorblind mode setting is never sent.

Where the data is kept

Account, progress and game data are kept on the servers of Supabase in São Paulo, Brazil, which processes them on our behalf and may log technical data such as your IP address. Settings and local high scores stay only on the device.

How to delete your account

In the game: Settings > Account > Delete account. Without the game: write to the contact below from your Google account email. Profile, games, ranking position and game data are erased immediately; backups, within 30 days.

Data collected by ads

We use Google AdMob to show ads. Google may collect and use:

  • the advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which allows estimating an approximate location;
  • device information such as model, system and language;
  • interactions with ads and diagnostic data.

This data is used to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where allowed, personalize ads. See how Google uses data and Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your mind in Settings > Ad privacy.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset the advertising ID in the device settings.
  • You can play without watching ads.
  • Sending detailed game data is optional. Change it in Settings > Send game data: when you turn it off, what was already sent is erased.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

Permissions

The game uses internet (account, progress, ranking and ads), network state (ads), the advertising ID (ads) and vibration. The ads library may also use basic connection information, keep the device awake and run tasks while ads are shown.

Your rights

Under the LGPD, the GDPR and similar laws, you may request information about your data, correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent. Write to the contact below. Requests about ad data are handled by Google; we can help direct them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
